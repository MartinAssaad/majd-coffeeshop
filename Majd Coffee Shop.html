About

Coffee with a sense of place

Majd sits in Tripoli's Saraya Al 'Atika, facing Akra and Al Koura Square. The café describes itself in one line: we combine coffee with heritage.

Guests on Google rate it 4.9 across 401 reviews. Come in for a coffee, stay for the room, or have it brought to you with delivery.
